# GenLayer Portal submission

## Title

RemedyRail — Consensus-backed SLA credits

## Project description

RemedyRail turns disputed SaaS outage reports into auditable invoice credits. A provider, customer and independent monitor approve immutable terms for a specific service, region and invoice line. The participants sign bounded observations, then GenLayer validators independently classify them against the terms and commit findings with exact evidence excerpts.

The contract derives eligible outage intervals from corroborated evidence, preserves both findings after a challenge and excludes double-counted minutes across cases. It issues only the incremental credit due under the committed invoice tiers. The provider records application of each memo and the customer signs an acknowledgment.

The dashboard includes signed evidence, interval-by-interval coverage, decision history, agreement and wallet controls, and an exportable audit bundle that recomputes settlement.
